Admin & user guide: updating Link to Care data

For M&E at Malaysian AIDS, Program Managers / Coordinators and authorised admins. Each tab in the database serves a specific purpose — follow the steps in order the first time, then use the side menu to jump straight to the tab you're working in.

1Check your Google profile

  1. Go to google.com and click your profile picture in the top-right corner.
  2. Make sure the profile shown is the account that was given access to hivnext.org. Usually this is the same email you use in MyVAS.
  3. If it isn't, switch to the correct account before opening any database or dashboard.

Some facilities manage their data with two emails. The Legacy system can only be shared with Google accounts, so a facility whose MyVAS email is not a Google account will have a separate Google email for Legacy.

2Open the Legacy portal

  1. From hivnext.org, choose the Legacy panel (or the Legacy link in the header on any page).
  2. In Access by NGO, find your NGO under its region.
  3. Click Database for the complete data of your NGO — this is restricted to PM/PC and admins who have been granted access.
  4. Click Dashboard for the NGO's latest achievements. Only users registered under that NGO can open its dashboard.

Legacy brings together data from 2024 through 2026. Other pages of the site are updated from time to time; check the changelogs for what's new.

3How the database is organised

To make the system more stable and faster — and to reduce fatigue when working with daily data — the whole structure was rebuilt. Data from 2024 and 2025, the user list and the treatment referral records now live in one Google Sheet per NGO.

Five tabs need attention from PM/PC and admins:

TabWhat it is for
actionTaskYour master pending list — every reactive HIV, SYP and HEP C case still awaiting Link to Care confirmation.
HIV, SYP, HEPCWhere you record the referral outcome for each disease category.
2024, 2025Historical records migrated from the previous system, with red cells that still need to be completed.

The other tabs (ipUser, dailyData, 2026) are managed by M&E and fill automatically.

4Tab: actionTask

The actionTask tab is the list of clients whose referral status needs updating. For 2026 clients it shows everyone who has not yet been referred to treatment.

  1. Open the actionTask tab in your NGO's database. You will see three column groups in different shades of green, as in the previous system: Pending HIV Referral, Pending SYP Referral and Pending HEPC Referral.
  2. Review each pending case. Each row is a client with a reactive result who has not been confirmed as linked to care. Check the IC number, test date and disease category.
  3. Act on each case by moving to the relevant tab — HIV, SYP or HEPC — to complete the Link to Care entry (step 5).

If a referral was already recorded in the previous system, you can copy the client's IC from this list and paste it into the HIV, SYP or HEPC tab instead of typing it.

5Tabs: HIV, SYP & HEPC

In the previous system every site had a separate Data-Rujukan-Rawatan file with four tabs (HIV, SYP/Syphilis, HEPC and PrEP). To make Link to Care updates easier, that file has been replaced by the HIV, SYP and HEPC tabs, which sit directly inside the same database. The workflow is identical in all three tabs.

  1. Open the tab for the disease category you are updating.
  2. In column A, open the drop-down. It lists the IC numbers of clients who have not yet been referred, taken from actionTask. Pick the client, or paste the IC you copied.
  3. Columns B to E fill in automatically once column A is set.
  4. Complete columns F to J: whether the client was referred (Yes / No), the referral date, the facility and the other referral details, and Notes in column J.

You only edit columns A, F, G, H, I and J in these three tabs. Everything else is calculated.

As soon as a client is marked Yes, they are removed from the actionTask list. If a case cannot yet be resolved — for example the client is unreachable — write the reason in Notes so the audit trail stays clean.

6MyHCC verification

Columns K and L show verification from the MyHCC system when a client has been successfully referred. Column K is also useful for checking whether a client already had a record at a Klinik Kesihatan before.

  • MyHCC verification is refreshed periodically — about once a month, not live.
  • For now it is available for HIV and HEPC only.

Because of this, it is important to keep Notes (column J) up to date when a client is a defaulter and the NGO is helping them restart treatment — especially for HIV cases. The note is what distinguishes a restart from a new referral.

7Tabs: 2024 & 2025

These tabs hold data migrated from the previous system. PM/PC also need to update treatment referral data here for clients who have still not been reached.

  1. Open the 2024 or 2025 tab.
  2. Look for cells marked red that are still empty. Only these need to be filled.
  3. The columns to complete are AN – AP, AS – AV and AW – AY.

Leave every other cell as it is — the rest of the row is the historical record.
