    How the model works

    Method, recommended splits and edge cases.

    Step by step (spreadsheet or code)

    1. Choose who is eligible: every CHW with scores in the award window (the last 6 months), including CHWs who have since left. New starters are scored on the months they served.
    2. Group them: by key population, everyone together, or custom groups, whichever M&E chooses. Each group is compared with its own members. Use the final score (0-100).
    3. Mean and standard deviation of each group: =AVERAGE(scores) and =STDEV.S(scores).
    4. Cut-offs: every tier except the last has a cut-off = mean + z × SD, where z can be negative and each cut-off is lower than the one above. A z of 1.04 is about the top 15%, 0 is the middle, and -1.28 is about the bottom 10%.
    5. Assign tiers: a CHW gets the first tier whose cut-off their score reaches, and the bottom tier below the last cut-off. For three tiers: =IF(score>=Cut1, 500, IF(score>=Cut2, 300, 50)).
    6. Fit to the budget: total cost = Σ (CHWs in tier × award). If it is over the available budget, scale all awards by budget ÷ cost and round down to the nearest RM5.
    7. Keep it internal: the rule is shared with M&E and MOH only. The leaderboard is for friendly competition, so CHWs never see awards.

    Edge cases

    • Several CHWs exactly on a cut-off: scores are compared at one decimal place, and anyone exactly on a cut-off goes to the higher tier. The total can then be slightly above plan.
    • Small groups: a group with fewer than the chosen size (10) uses percentile rank instead, because its mean and SD are unreliable. With fewer than 3 CHWs everyone gets the award of the middle tier.
    • Many CHWs at 100: scores are capped, so the curve is not truly normal and the real top share can exceed the target. That is fair, since each earned it. Use percentile rank for a fixed budget.
    • Skewed scores: compare the target split with the actual split in the table above. If they differ a lot, prefer percentile rank.
    • Leavers and joiners: everyone scored in the window is included, even if they have since left. Anyone who joined later is scored on the months they served.
    • Budget: the model never pays more than the available budget when “Scale down” or “Use the full budget” is on.

    Scoring settings

    M&E only. Change the targets and weights used to calculate scores.

    Edits are held here until you click Apply changes, so the leaderboard recalculates once, not on every keystroke. In production, Apply saves the settings (logged with who and when) and scores use them from the next monthly refresh.

    How scoring works

    The weight of each indicator, for every key population.
    • Your score is out of 100. Each indicator earns up to its weight, and is capped at 100% of its target.
    • Targets are prorated by months in service, so new CHWs are not penalised.
    • Rankings refresh on the 10th of every month and cover closed months only.
